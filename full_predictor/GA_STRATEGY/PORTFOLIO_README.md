# Portfolio Replay Engine: Top-Stock Cash Allocation Simulator

The `portfolio.py` runner replays saved monthly walk-forward selections from `batch.py` into a realistic, cash-constrained delivery portfolio with **NSE circuit-breaker filters** and **cross-sectional momentum selection**.

---

## 1. Quick Start

Run from the `full_predictor` directory:

```powershell
python -m GA_STRATEGY.portfolio \
    --results reports/composite_long_5years/monthly_results.csv \
    --capital 1000000 \
    --allocation 0.04 \
    --top 10 \
    --output reports/top10_portfolio
```

---

## 2. Core Simulation Mechanics

### Cash Ledger & Capital Allocation
* **Starting Capital:** Default ₹1,000,000 cash balance.
* **No Margin or Shorting:** Cash-funded delivery equity portfolio. Cash cannot go negative.
* **Position Sizing:** Each entry notional is capped at **4% of total marked portfolio equity** at that timestamp (`allocation = 0.04` $\approx$ ₹40,000 on ₹10L equity), rounded down to whole shares and limited by available cash.
* **One Position per Stock:** Re-entries while already holding a stock are ignored until the existing position is closed.
* **Execution Delay:** Strategy signals determined at bar $t$ Close execute at the **next bar's Open** ($t+1$).
* **Transaction Friction:** Every buy and sell incurs a **0.05%** fee (`DEFAULT_COST = 0.0005`).

### Trailing 3-Month Cross-Sectional Ranking
* Before each calendar month, the engine compounds the out-of-sample returns from the **preceding three consecutive calendar months** for all stocks.
* Stocks are sorted in descending order of trailing 3-month return.
* Only the **Top N** (e.g. Top 10) stocks are **eligible** to open new positions during that calendar month.
* Stocks with missing months in the 3-month lookback window are disqualified.
* Stocks that fall out of the Top N have their open positions closed at the next observed open.

---

## 3. Exchange Circuit-Breaker Handling (NSE Realism)

In real Indian stock exchanges (NSE/BSE), stocks frequently hit daily circuit bands (5%, 10%, 20%). The simulator eliminates unrealistic "paper profits" and models true slippage:

### Upper Circuit (UC) - Zero Sellers
* **Condition:** $\text{Daily Return} \ge +4.8\%$ and flat price range ($\text{High} == \text{Low}$).
* **Execution:** **All BUY orders are canceled/skipped.** In real markets, there are only buyers and zero sellers, so buy orders cannot be filled.

### Lower Circuit (LC) - Trapped Position Modeling
* **Condition:** $\text{Daily Return} \le -4.8\%$ and flat price range ($\text{High} == \text{Low}$).
* **Execution:** **SELL orders cannot execute immediately.** In real markets, there are only sellers and zero buyers. The position remains held, takes daily mark-to-market drops, and **only executes when the circuit unlocks** with normal trading volume.
* **No Lower Circuit Buys:** BUY orders during lower circuits are blocked to prevent catching falling knives on frozen limit bars.

## 5. Generated Outputs

* **`portfolio_value.png`:** Plot of total marked portfolio value vs. cash balance over time.
* **`portfolio_value.csv`:** Daily time series recording `date, equity, cash, invested, positions`.
* **`executed_trades.csv`:** Full audit trail of every fill with timestamp, stock, side, shares, fill price, fee, cash balance, and execution notes (`BUY`, `NORMAL_SELL`, `LC_UNLOCKED_EXIT`).
* **`monthly_stock_ranks.csv`:** Historical scores, eligibility status, and monthly ranks for all candidate stocks.
* **`candidate_activity.csv`:** Signal change counts and eligibility flags for each stock-month.
* **`summary.json`:** Concise summary containing final equity, cumulative return, daily max drawdown, total fees, and fill count.

## FYERS transaction charges

The default portfolio model uses the current Standard NSE equity-intraday tariff checked on 2026-11-16, applied to historical prices. See https://fyers.in/charges-list and https://fyers.in/pricing.

Each simulated fill is one fully executed order. Brokerage is the lower of INR 20 and 0.03% of turnover. Exchange charges are 0.0030699%, SEBI charges INR 10 per crore, and IPFT INR 0.01 per crore on both sides. STT is 0.025% on sells. Stamp duty is 0.003% on buys. GST is 18% of brokerage, exchange, SEBI, and IPFT charges, following the detailed tariff page. No separate clearing fee is assumed for cash equity.

Components are rounded to paise per executed order using decimal half-up rounding. The result is a current-tariff estimate. Exact contract-note aggregation and statutory rounding remain unverified. An FYERS support article differs on GST treatment of SEBI fees. The detailed tariff page supplies this implementation's GST base.

Executed trades contain order IDs and each charge component. daily_charges.csv aggregates those order charges by day. Summary JSON includes the tariff description and totals. Affordability uses the entire buy charge, including capped brokerage.

Scheduled strategy square-off incurs ordinary sell charges. The fee helper can additionally calculate an INR 50 administrative-square-off charge plus GST, but the simulator does not assume a broker administrative intervention occurred. If a lower circuit or missing exit bar prevents same-day closure, the simulator assumes delivery conversion. It adjusts buy brokerage to min(INR 20, 0.3%), buy STT to 0.1%, and buy stamp duty to 0.015%. On exit, it uses delivery brokerage, 0.1% sell STT and INR 12.50 DP charges plus applicable GST. Conversion fee differences appear as FEE rows. overnight_positions.csv records each carried stock-day. Broker conversion acceptance and auction/settlement penalties are not modeled. Additional mandatory conversion charges can create a cash debit if the account has insufficient reserves.

Defaults for the entry cutoff and square-off time are constants in portfolio.py. Existing batch scores and stock ranking inputs still use their saved fee assumptions. Reproducing historical tariffs requires effective-dated fee schedules.

## Leverage and margin accounting

Default leverage is 1. Set --leverage 5 to assume up to 5x intraday exposure for the scenario. Actual eligibility is stock-specific and can change over time. --leverage-file accepts a JSON object such as {"ABB": 5, "CGPOWER": 1}. Unlisted stocks use --leverage. These settings are assumptions, with no automatic historical margin-data lookup.

--allocation-mode notional, the default, caps position notional at allocation times equity. --allocation-mode margin caps entry margin at allocation times equity, allowing a notional up to allocation times equity times the stock leverage. Fee-inclusive available margin can reduce that quantity. With INR 10 lakh, allocation 0.12 and leverage 5, margin mode targets up to INR 6 lakh notional using INR 1.2 lakh margin. Fees apply to the full traded notional.

The cash column records unblocked cash after fees and realized gains or losses. Blocked margin is reserved at entry. Financed is the accounting offset between entry notional and blocked margin. Equity equals free cash plus market value of holdings minus financed amounts. Gross exposure, blocked margin, financed amounts and available margin are exported. Unrealized losses reduce available margin. Unrealized gains cannot increase the free cash available for new entries before realization.

If marked equity falls below required entry margin, the simulator records a margin event and requests exits, retrying at observed unlocked bars. Leverage runs check every observed market timestamp. A blocked overnight conversion removes intraday financing and requires full funding. Insufficient funds appear as a cash debit in margin_events.csv. Interest on such debits and broker-specific liquidation rules remain unmodeled.

Example from full_predictor:

```powershell
python -m GA_STRATEGY.portfolio --results reports/calmar_long_5years_full/monthly_results.csv --capital 1000000 --allocation 0.12 --leverage 5 --allocation-mode margin --top 10 --output reports/portfolio_calmar_5x
```
